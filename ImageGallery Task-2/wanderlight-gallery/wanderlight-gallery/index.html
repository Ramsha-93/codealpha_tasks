About the journal

Looking closely, one plate at a time.

Wanderlight started as a set of notebooks kept on long train rides and slower mornings — a way of paying attention to valleys, buildings, animals, and street corners that would otherwise pass by unrecorded.

Every plate on this wall is chosen for what it teaches about light and patience, not for where it was taken. The categories are loose; the attention behind them isn't.

There's no studio here, no controlled light — just a bag, a notebook, and a habit of arriving early and leaving late. Most plates are the fourth or fifth attempt at the same corner, on a day the light finally cooperated.

Field notes
2019

The first notebook

Started keeping a paper journal of light and weather on weekend train trips — no camera worth mentioning, just notes.

2021

Wanderlight goes public

Turned three years of notebooks into a small online wall of plates — landscapes and architecture first, wildlife later.

2023

Street work begins

Added a fourth category after a month spent shooting nothing but market mornings and rain-lit alleyways.

2026

Forty-eight plates, and counting

The wall keeps growing steadily — a dozen new plates a season, each one still chosen by hand.
